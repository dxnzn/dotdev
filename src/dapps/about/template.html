Q // What is dnzn.wei and the Wei Name Service?

Wei Name Service (WNS) is an alternative to the Ethereum Name Service (ENS). Like .eth, a .wei name resolves to an ethereum address allowing you to own your on-chain identity.

WNS uniquely supports 1 & 2 character names (like z.wei or hi.wei), and registration is significantly cheaper than ENS. Each registered namespace is an NFT that you own, and can be sold or transfered like any other NFT.

The WNS Resolver project offers a Chrome extension that detects Ethereum addresses on any page and resolves them to their .wei primary names in-place.

Q // What framework is this site built on?

This site is built on DxKit — A headless microframework for building composable dapps. It offers routing, lifecycle management, event bus, plugin registry — with zero DOM ownership.

The entire site is vanilla TypeScript compiled via tsup, and no other frameworks or bundler runtime. Each page is a standalone dapp with its own manifest, HTML template, and lifecycle. The shell handles chrome (header, nav, theme switching, footer) while DxKit handles the orchestration underneath. The result is a site that loads fast, is trivially extensible, and deploys anywhere (GitHub Pages, IPFS, etc).

Q // Who are the little faces and what are zOrgz?

zOrgz are an NFT collection created by z0r0z — the creator of zFi — as an on-chain art experiment exploring minimal generative identity on Ethereum.

We've adopted the zOrgz faces as our temporal visual identity to show continued support for zFi and it's broader ecosystem. The zFi Superdapp is what inspired us to build DxKit, and a lot of our interest for twenty-twentysix is in finding, following, and supporting the builders who are creating cool shit.

We encourage others to join the zOrg DAO and become part of the collective.

Q // What does "distrust |&| verify" mean?

At DNZN // RESEARCH we do detailed smart contract analysis, breakdowns of technology and concepts, and anything else that we care about.

The saying "distrust |&| verify" is a play on "trust, but verify", and organically became our ethos as we began doing deeper research on projects we are interested in where often the developers are undoxxed, and the contracts we are attempting to understand are only available as unverified bytecode on-chain.
